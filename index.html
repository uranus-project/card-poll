Frequently asked questions

What is Uranus Project?

Uranus Project (working name) is a new way to play YGO. A way that's accessible and fun, that's beginner-friendly and interesting, that's new and nostalgic at the same time.

Most importantly: Uranus Project is a free fan game. It is not affiliated with Konami in any way.

Some huge inspirations for Uranus Project are Yu-Gi-Oh! Rush and Pokémon TCG Pocket.

What is this poll?

This poll is a way to source from you, the players, cards that make you think of the game. They don't have to be old, they don't have to be new. Is your favorite card Marshmallon? Is it Mystic Mine (hello, Jeff Leonard)? Is it I:P Masquerena? Everything goes.

You don't have to choose only one card, and they don't necessarily have to be your favorite cards. Mystical Space Typhoon is maybe no one's favorite card, but it sure makes you think of YGO, doesn't it?

Why can't I find a card?

If you are looking for a card but it's not on the list, good news: it means that's a card that makes someone else think of YGO as well! Every time a card is submitted, it is removed from the available options.

We are not looking for a popularity contest: getting a thousand people to vote for Blue-Eyes White Dragon does not really help. We want you to go fringe, to help us find cards that we didn't think of. Everyone's experience with YGO is different, and we want to hear from all of you!

How can I stay updated on Uranus Project?

We currently don't want to collect your contact information, so there is no way to stay truly updated. However, we trust that if you are involved in the YGO community and you got this poll the first time, you will probably come across news about it in the future.

If you truly want to make sure you don't miss any further news, bookmark this page (or uranus-project.github.io) and check back on it later in 2027!
